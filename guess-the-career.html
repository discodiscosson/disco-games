Guess the Career: the football career guessing game

You get a footballer's career table with clubs, years, appearances and goals. Can you work out who it is before the 20 seconds run out?

Play Guess the Career

How to play

  1. Read the career table. It looks like the career box on a Wikipedia player page: the years, the clubs, and the appearances and goals for each.
  2. Type a name. Suggestions appear as you type, matching both first names and surnames. Accents are not needed, so "Muller" matches "Müller".
  3. Beat the timer. You have 20 seconds per player. Leave the box empty and press Enter to skip.
  4. Build a streak. Every correct guess in a row adds to your streak. A wrong guess, a skip or a timeout ends it.
  5. Keep going. Players you have solved do not come back, so you can work towards completing whole leagues and climb the leaderboard.

Features

  • About 2,800 playersFrom six leagues, with the full career table for each player.
  • Filter by league and clubPick whole leagues or individual clubs, such as only Premier League and La Liga sides. Choose at least five clubs for the filter to apply.
  • Easy ModeOnly well-known players, for a gentler start or a quick game.
  • Streak badgesEarn ⚽ at 3, 🔥 at 5, 🌟 at 7 and 🐐 at 10 correct guesses in a row.
  • LeaderboardCompete worldwide or within a league or club. Click a name to see someone's three best leagues.
  • Easiest and hardest playerSee which players the community finds easiest and hardest to identify.

Tips for better guesses

  • Start with the years. The dates place the player in an era, and the clubs usually narrow it down quickly.
  • Check goals against appearances. A high goals-to-games ratio points to a striker, and a very low one to a defender or goalkeeper.
  • Look for loans. Rows marked with an arrow and "(loan)" show spells at other clubs, which are often the most distinctive clues.
  • Notice reserve teams. Names like "II" or "B" are reserve sides, which show where a player came through as a youngster.
  • Use the suggestions. If you only remember a surname, start typing it and pick from the list.

Fair play and your account

Fair leaderboards

Answers are checked on the server and are never sent to your browser. Scores, streaks and rankings are calculated by the server too, so the leaderboard reflects what players actually guessed.

Guest or signed in

You can play as a guest straight away. If you sign in with Google, your guest progress comes with you and you appear on the leaderboard. You can delete your account and all your data yourself from your profile. See the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Guess the Career free?

Yes, it is free to play and there are no ads at the moment.

Do I need to sign in?

No. You can play as a guest and your progress is saved on your device. Sign in with Google to keep your progress across devices and to appear on the leaderboard.

Where does the career data come from?

The data is compiled from Wikipedia and shown in a similar format to the career box on a player's page.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game runs in your mobile browser, so there is nothing to download.

How is the leaderboard ranked?

By the number of different players you have identified in the selected scope (worldwide, a league or a club). Ties are ordered by who joined first.

What is Easy Mode?

Easy Mode only serves well-known players from major clubs. League and club filters are turned off while it is on.

Why is my favourite player or club missing?

The game covers six leagues for now, and clubs and leagues are added gradually. Send suggestions to info@disco.games.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in, open your profile and choose "Delete account". This permanently removes your account, progress and leaderboard entry.

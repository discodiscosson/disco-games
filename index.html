About disco.games

disco.games is an independent project made by David Larsson in Sweden. The idea is simple: games that are quick to start, easy to understand and hard to put down, for people who enjoy trivia and sports.

Each game lives on its own address, such as guessthecareer.disco.games, so it loads fast and can be improved without affecting the others.

  • Free and instantPlay in your browser on any modern phone, tablet or computer.
  • Fair leaderboardsAnswers are checked on the server, so scores and rankings can be trusted.
  • Privacy-mindedNo advertising or tracking cookies today. You can play as a guest, and delete your account yourself at any time.

Frequently asked questions

Are the games free?

Yes. All games on disco.games are free to play. There are no ads at the moment.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start playing as a guest and your progress is saved on your device. If you sign in with Google, your guest progress follows you and you can join the leaderboard.

Which devices and browsers work?

The games run in current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, on phones, tablets and computers. Nothing needs to be installed.

What happens to my data?

We only store what is needed to run the game: your account, display name, progress and streaks. Read the full privacy policy. You can delete your account and all your data from your profile in the game.

How can I contact you?

Email info@disco.games. Suggestions for new games and leagues are welcome.

Flag Quiz: guess the country from its flag

See a flag, name the country before the 20-second timer runs out, and work your way through all 197 countries.

Play Flag Quiz How to play

How to play

  1. A flag appears on the screen.
  2. Type the country’s name and pick it from the list.
  3. Answer within 20 seconds. Get it right and the flag is yours. Get it wrong and it goes to the back of the pile, so you meet it again later.
  4. Keep going until you have found every flag in your selection, and build streaks along the way.

What's in the game

  • All 197 countriesGrouped by continent, so you can practise one region at a time.
  • Regional flagsGo beyond countries with flags such as the US states.
  • Free flagsPlay at least one guess on 5 days in a row to earn a free flag that reveals one you are stuck on. You can save up to 3.
  • LeaderboardsSee how you rank worldwide and on each continent.
  • One account for all gamesPlay as a guest, or sign in with Google to save your progress across every game on disco.games.

Tips

Start with the continent you know least. Because wrong answers come back later in the same round, you learn the hard flags by repetition.

Many flags share colours and stripes. Look for the details: an emblem, a star or the order of the stripes is often what tells two flags apart.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Flag Quiz free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no ads at the moment.

How many flags are there?

All 197 countries, plus regional flags such as the US states.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest straight away. Sign in with Google to save your progress and join the leaderboard.

Where do the flag images come from?

The flags come from flag-icons, an open-source flag collection under the MIT licence.

Guess the Airport: the airport quiz played on runways

All you get is the runways. Read the layout and the runway numbers, then name the airport before the 20-second timer runs out.

Play Guess the Airport How to play

How to play

  1. You see the runways of a mystery airport, drawn to scale with north up and the runway numbers at each end.
  2. Type the airport’s name, its city or its code, and pick the right one from the list.
  3. Answer within 20 seconds. A correct answer adds the airport to your collection. Miss it and it goes back into the pile for later.
  4. Answer correctly several times in a row to build a streak and earn badges at 3, 5, 7 and 10.

What's in the game

  • Around 300 airportsThe world’s biggest airports, from every continent.
  • Pick your regionFocus on one or more continents, or play the whole world.
  • LeaderboardsSee how you rank worldwide and on each continent. Your score is the number of airports you have found.
  • One account for all gamesPlay as a guest, or sign in with Google to save your progress across every game on disco.games.

Tips

Runway numbers are compass headings divided by ten. A runway marked 09/27 runs east–west, and 18/36 runs north–south.

L, C and R mark parallel runways: left, centre and right. Two or three parallel runways narrow the list down quickly.

Look at the overall shape. Crossing runways, triangles and long parallel pairs are often more telling than the exact numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Is Guess the Airport free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no ads at the moment.

Where does the airport data come from?

Runway data comes from OurAirports, a public-domain database of airports around the world.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest straight away. Sign in with Google to save your progress and join the leaderboard.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The game runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.
